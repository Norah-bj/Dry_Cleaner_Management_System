The new system is almost ready for the shop. **You enter your prices,

We only need answers to the questions below, which the system can't

## 1. EBM (tax receipts)

**1a.** Which EBM machine or software do you use? (The brand or name, or a
photo of the machine or of a receipt, helps.)

**1b.** Should the new system print the EBM receipt, or will you keep using
the EBM machine separately and enter each sale there?

## 2. Cancellations, refunds and damage

The system can already give discounts if you turn them on in Settings.
These situations need a decision from you:

**2a.** If a customer cancels an order, what happens to money already paid?
(Refund it all? Keep part of it? Keep it as credit?)

**2b.** If clothes are damaged or lost, what do you do today?

**2c.** If a customer never collects their clothes, how long do you keep
them, and what happens after that?

## 3. Delivery

**3a.** Which areas do you deliver to?

**3b.** Do you charge for pickup or delivery? If yes, is it one price or
does it depend on the distance?

**3c.** Does each driver have a smartphone with internet? (Drivers see
their jobs on their phone.)

## 4. Messages to customers

**4a.** How do you want to tell customers their clothes are ready?
- [ ] WhatsApp
- [ ] SMS
- [ ] Phone call
- [ ] No message

**4b.** Do you have, or plan to get, a WhatsApp Business API account?
(Most small shops don't. If you don't, the first version will open a
WhatsApp chat with your number.)

## 5. Existing records

**5a.** Do you have customer records today (a notebook, Excel, phone
contacts)? Do you want them added to the new system before you start?

**5b.** Are there orders in the shop right now that should be in the system
on day one?
